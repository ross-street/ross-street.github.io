\begin{equation}
\xymatrix{
X \ar[rr]^-{f} \ar[d]_-{g} && Y \ar[d]^-{h} \\
Z \ar[rr]_-{k} && W}
\end{equation}

This is a commutative pentagon:

\begin{equation}
\xymatrix{
& Y \ar[rd]^-{ g}  & \\
X \ar[ru]^-{f} \ar[d]_-{u} & & Z \ar[d]^-{h} \\
V \ar[rr]_-{v} & & W }
\end{equation}

Playing around:

\begin{equation}
