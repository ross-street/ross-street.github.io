Macquarie University Mathematics Department

VACATION SCHOLARSHIPS IN MATHEMATICS

January -- February 2007

  • Applications close Friday 24 November 2006
  • Available to first, second and third year students (in 2006), with good results in mathematics either at Macquarie or at another university, who intend to continue with further mathematics units in the next year (for third year students this would mean they would be applying for Mathematics Honours in 2007)
  • Scholarship holders will carry out a minor research project under the guidance of a supervisor
  • Each scholarship is for $300 per week (tax free)
  • Scholarships are for a period of 4 weeks full time within the long vacation; actual dates are negotiated between scholarship holder and supervisor
  • Awards are primarily on the basis of exam performance
  • Application form
