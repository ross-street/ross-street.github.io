Awards

Stephen Lack was awarded the Medal of the Australian Mathematical Society for 2009.

Ross Street was awarded the Chaire de la Vallée Poisson for 2011 by the ancient Catholic University of Louvain, Belgium, and a conference was organized in connection with the Chaire.

Dominic Verity received the Macquarie University 2008 Vice Chancellor's Award for Teaching Excellence.

Michael Johnson chaired the AMAST (Algebraic Methodology and Software Technology) conference held in Quebec City in June and lectured at the University of Milan in April.

Stephen Lack was appointed chair of the Scientific Committee for the major international category theory conference CT2011 at UBC, Vancouver, Canada.
